About Me

Hi, I'm Aadesh. I'm the founding engineer at Tailored AI, where I build AI systems that have to work in production: agents, voice, retrieval, and the evaluation loops around them. Before that, an MTech in Computer Science & Information Security at IIIT Hyderabad. If you want the war stories, they're in the journal; this page is the rest of me.

The short version of how I think about work: the model is the easy part. Most of what I've learned came from the unglamorous layers around it, and from getting things wrong first. I'd rather ship something boring that holds than something impressive that doesn't, and I try to write the way I work: specific, honest about what I don't know, allergic to hype.

Off the Clock

I'm happiest on a mountain trail with a camera in my bag; some of what comes back ends up in the gallery. I read slowly and widely, and keep notes on it over on the books page. And for about four years before my Master's, I spent my Saturdays teaching mathematics, physics, and programming to shelter-home students in Pune. Still some of the most useful hours I've spent with another person's attention.
